Eight calm ways to clear your camera roll

Each module is honest about what it's doing and why. Nothing auto-deletes; nothing pretends to be magic.

Exact duplicates

Byte-for-byte identical copies, found in seconds. Apple's built-in catches some — Bide catches imported re-copies too.

Screen recordings

Usually the biggest disposable files in your library. One module just for these so the reclaim is obvious.

Large videos

Sorted by size. Tap to preview, decide, move to Recently Deleted. Favorites and hidden videos are always protected.

Screenshots

Grouped by month and year. Tap "Sort by type" to bucket into chat / app / visual using on-device OCR. Pick a year and clear all of it.

Live Photos

Each one carries a video sidecar that adds roughly 40% to its size. Surface the biggest ones first so you can decide deliberately.

Similar photos

Time-bucketed clusters with a suggested keeper and the reason ("favorited", "highest resolution", "edited"). You decide.

Blurry shots

Conservative candidates only. Vision face detection protects portraits — slightly blurry photos of people stay in your library.

On this day

Photos from this calendar day across past years. A calm five-minute reason to open Bide every day — not just when storage gets tight.

Frequently asked

The questions people most often ask before they trust a cleaner app.

Does Bide upload my photos anywhere?

No. All analysis — clustering similar photos, blur detection, OCR for screenshot categorization, face detection that protects portraits — runs on your iPhone's Neural Engine. There's no backend server we control. The only diagnostics involved come from Apple's MetricKit framework, which stays on your device and which you can read in Bide's Settings → Diagnostics.

Does Bide delete photos automatically?

Never. Every removal goes through a Review Basket and a confirmation step. Items move to Apple's Recently Deleted album, where Photos keeps them for 30 days before permanent deletion. Bide doesn't have a "Delete forever" mode and doesn't reach into Recently Deleted.

Can I recover something I removed by accident?

Yes — open Photos, scroll to Albums, tap Recently Deleted, and tap Recover. You have 30 days from the moment Bide moved the item before iOS permanently removes it. Bide's session-summary screen has a direct shortcut to that album.

Why does Bide need photo library access?

To find clutter we have to be able to see your photos. We request read and write access — read so we can analyze, write so we can move items to Recently Deleted on your behalf. You can choose Limited Access to give Bide a subset; the app supports this honestly with a banner noting scans will be partial.

How is Bide different from the Duplicates album in Photos?

Apple's built-in tool finds exact-byte duplicates and lets you merge them — useful, but narrow. Bide adds:

  • Near-duplicates (similar bursts, near-identical shots from the same scene) via Vision feature prints
  • Screen recordings as a distinct, fast-clear category
  • Screenshots organized by month, year, and content type
  • Blurry-shot candidates with face protection
  • A daily "On this day" review for ongoing maintenance
  • A Review Basket so nothing happens accidentally
Is Bide really free? What's the catch?

There's no catch. No ads. No premium tier. No "Pro" upsell. No data sale. The source code is public on GitHub so you can verify. The category — iPhone "cleaner apps" — is dominated by predatory subscription gates and scare-tactic marketing; Bide is what one of them should look like.

Does Bide work with iCloud Photos?

Yes. We never auto-download from iCloud (that would burn your cellular data and battery). Items that aren't downloaded locally show a "Tap to load" affordance with an iCloud icon — your tap is the consent to download for that specific asset.

What's the deal with the name?

"Bide" means to dwell, to wait patiently. It's the entire product philosophy in one syllable — take your time, no rush, look twice before letting anything go. We named the app for the feeling, not the function. The category is full of "Smart Cleaner" and "AI Cleanup"; we're the opposite of that.
